## 📋 Eligibility Requirements

### Technical Prerequisites

- **Blockchain Experience**: Minimum 2 years of blockchain development experience
- **Programming Skills**: Proficiency in Rust, Solidity, or similar smart contract languages
- **Security Knowledge**: Understanding of cryptographic principles and security best practices
- **Innovation Focus**: Commitment to building quantum-resistant solutions

### Project Criteria

- **Unique Value Proposition**: Clear differentiation from existing solutions
- **Market Potential**: Demonstrated demand and user adoption potential
- **Technical Feasibility**: Realistic development timeline and resource requirements
- **Quantum Integration**: Plans for implementing quantum-resistant features

### Team Requirements

- **Dedicated Team**: Committed full-time or substantial part-time development team
- **Technical Leadership**: Experienced lead developer with blockchain expertise
- **Business Acumen**: Understanding of tokenomics and sustainable business models
- **Community Engagement**: Willingness to participate in ARMChain ecosystem activities
