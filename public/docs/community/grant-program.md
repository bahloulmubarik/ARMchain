Our Grant Program is a comprehensive funding initiative designed to accelerate the development of the ARMChain ecosystem. We provide financial support, technical resources, and strategic guidance to projects that align with our mission of creating a quantum-secure blockchain future.

### Grant Program Mission

- **🔬 Innovation**: Fund cutting-edge research in quantum-resistant technologies

## 📊 Grant Application Process

### Phase 1: Initial Proposal (2 weeks review)

**Required Documentation:**
- Executive summary and project overview
- Detailed technical specification and architecture
- Team background and relevant experience
- Budget breakdown and funding justification
- Timeline with major milestones and deliverables
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- **Innovation Score (25%)**: Novelty and technical advancement
- **Team Capability (25%)**: Experience and track record
- **Market Impact (20%)**: Potential adoption and ecosystem value
- **Technical Feasibility (20%)**: Realistic timeline and resource requirements
- **Alignment (10%)**: Fit with ARMChain's strategic objectives

### Phase 2: Technical Review (3 weeks review)

**Deep Technical Assessment:**
- Architecture review by ARMChain core developers
- Security analysis and quantum-resistance evaluation
- Scalability and performance projections
- Integration compatibility with existing ARMChain infrastructure

**Team Interview Process:**
- Technical presentation to ARMChain engineering team
- Code review of previous projects and proof-of-concepts
- Problem-solving exercises and technical discussions
- Cultural fit assessment and communication evaluation

### Phase 3: Final Approval (2 weeks review)

**Strategic Alignment Review:**
- Business model evaluation and sustainability analysis
- Partnership potential and ecosystem integration opportunities
- Competitive landscape analysis and differentiation factors
- Long-term strategic value for ARMChain ecosystem

**Due Diligence Process:**
- Legal entity verification and compliance check
- Financial background and funding history review
- Reference checks with previous collaborators and investors
- Intellectual property analysis and licensing considerations

## 📋 Funding Structure & Terms

### Milestone-Based Payments

**Research Grants:**
- 40% upon project initiation and team onboarding
- 30% at mid-point research milestone completion
- 30% upon final deliverable submission and peer review

**Development Grants:**
- 25% upon project kickoff and initial setup
- 25% at alpha version completion and testing
- 25% at beta release and security audit completion
- 25% at mainnet deployment and ecosystem integration

### Grant Terms & Conditions

**Intellectual Property:**
- Open-source licensing required for publicly funded components
- ARMChain receives non-exclusive license for ecosystem integration
- Commercial applications retain proprietary rights with revenue sharing
- Patent applications must acknowledge ARMChain grant support

**Reporting Requirements:**
- Monthly progress reports with technical and financial updates
- Quarterly strategic reviews with ARMChain ecosystem team
- Annual impact assessment and ecosystem contribution analysis
- Final project report with open-source code and documentation

**Performance Metrics:**
- Technical milestones completion rate (minimum 85%)
- Budget adherence and financial management (±10% variance)
- Community engagement and adoption metrics
- Code quality standards and security audit results
